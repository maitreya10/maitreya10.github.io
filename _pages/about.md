I am a first-year PhD student in the Division of Electrical and Computer Engineering at [Louisiana State University](https://www.lsu.edu/eng/ece/index.php) advised by [Dr. Xugui Zhou](https://xugui-zhou.github.io/). My research lies at the intersection of Security and Machine Learning, with applications in Autonomous Driving. I hold a Master’s degree in Information Systems Management from [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.heinz.cmu.edu/), and a B.E. in Computer Science from [Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering](https://computer.pccoepune.com/) (Savitribai Phule Pune University).
